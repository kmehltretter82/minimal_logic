Definition layer
The indexed Realizer carrier has exactly the source's shape alternatives:
epsilon at *, a raw term otherwise, together with a separate typing predicate.
It does not fabricate a term at an absent realizer for X. The term language,
formula classes, type assignment, comprehension substitution, and structural
extraction remain the published ones. New realizability renames source-bound
variables before translating, keeping existing realizer and comprehension
parameters fixed. The maximum-identifier construction avoids every variable
already present, not only free variables. Irrelevant formulas are returned up
to alpha conversion; the original calculus explicitly permits that conversion.

Theorem 4.1
The hypotheses say that the given proof is derivable and that the dummy is typed
and closed, assumption labels are formula-consistent, and their identifiers are
separate from object identifiers in the proof. The last two are explicit naming
conventions for the source's distinct variable classes. Both old restrictions
on comprehension parameters have been removed. The conclusion uses the exact
structural extraction, proves its typing, and permits only translated source
assumptions in the target derivation. It does not require every source assumption
to be used; this agrees with derivability from a set of assumptions.
